Specialising in Labour Law

A labour law attorney (sometimes called an employment lawyer) specialises in the laws and regulations that govern the relationship between employers, employees, and trade unions. Their work covers workplace rights, duties, and disputes.

Employment Contracts

Drafting and reviewing employment contracts and workplace policies. Ensuring compliance with labour legislation (e.g., Basic Conditions of Employment Act in South Africa).

Workplace Disputes

Representing employers or employees in disputes over unfair dismissal, retrenchments, or workplace grievances.Handling disputes before the Commission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration (CCMA), Bargaining Councils, or Labour Courts.

Unfair Labour Practices

Cases involving discrimination, harassment, or unfair treatment at work. Advising on disciplinary hearings, suspensions, or warnings.

Trade Union & Collective Bargaining Issues

Assisting with strikes, lockouts, and union negotiations. Advising employers and unions on collective agreements.

Health & Safety and Compliance

nsuring workplaces comply with occupational health and safety laws. Advising on employer liability in cases of workplace injuries or accidents.

Retrenchments & Restructuring

Guiding businesses through retrenchment processes. Ensuring fair procedures are followed to avoid legal consequences
